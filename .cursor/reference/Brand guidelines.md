# Brand Guidelines

## Color Palette

The CarryMe color system follows the 60-30-10 rule: a dominant primary, a supporting secondary, and accent colors used sparingly for emphasis and contrast.

| Role | Hex | Usage Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | `#F34213` | 60% | Dominant brand color. Use for primary surfaces, key UI containers, and main brand expressions. |
| **Secondary** | `#BEB7A4` | 30% | Supporting color. Use for secondary surfaces, primary CTAs, links, and active states. |
| **Accent** | `#FFFFFC` | 10% (shared) | Bright accent. Use for highlights, badges, success/info cues, and small interactive flourishes. |
| **Accent** | `#000000` | 10% (shared) | Deep accent. Use for text on light surfaces, dark mode backgrounds, footers, and high-contrast emphasis. |

### Swatches

- Primary — `#F34213`
- Secondary — `#BEB7A4`
- Accent (Light) — `#FFFFFC`
- Accent (Deep) — `#000000`

### Usage Rules

- Keep the **60 / 30 / 10** distribution across any single screen, marketing asset, or layout.
- The two accent colors share the 10% accent allocation — do not exceed a combined 10% in any composition.
- Never place `#BEB7A4` directly on `#FFFFFC` (insufficient contrast); 
- Use `#102E4A` for body text on light backgrounds; use white for body text on `#715AFF` or `#102E4A` backgrounds.
